How Viewer Card Pulling Works

Viewers interact with your extension directly beneath your stream in the About Panels or via the Stream Overlay on the video player.

  • Choose Any Active Cardset: All non-vaulted cardsets are live simultaneously—viewers pick which set to pull from.
  • Channel Point Packs: Pull 1 Card (2,000 Pts) or a 3-Card Pack (4,000 Pts), or open gifts sent by the broadcaster.
  • Duplicate → Chimbles Crafting: Duplicate pulls convert into Chimbles to unlock hidden uncollected cards (even in vaulted sets).
  • Set Badges: Complete 100% of any cardset to unlock its exclusive completion badge in your binder.
